## A little about me

I'm in my second year of CSE (Artificial Intelligence). I started out spending a lot of time with Java and DSA, and lately I've been getting more interested in how complete applications work — not just the algorithm, but the APIs, data, and decisions around it.

I like building a rough first version, figuring out why parts of it don't work, and improving it from there. Most of my projects are still evolving, and I prefer showing that process rather than pretending everything is finished.
